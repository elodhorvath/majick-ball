# Installing the Majick Ball on Your iPad or iPhone

**The short version: open the link, tap the Share button, choose "Add to Home
Screen." Done.**

You'll do this once. After that, the ball works even with no internet at all —
at the tournament, in airplane mode, wherever.

## The links

- **Magic 8-Ball** (Jamie):
  <https://elodhorvath.github.io/majick-ball/magic-8ball.html>
- **Magic 9-Ball** (Elod):
  <https://elodhorvath.github.io/majick-ball/magic-9ball.html>

## Step by step

1. **On the iPad or iPhone, open Safari.** (The compass icon. It must be
   Safari — the other browsers can't do the "Add to Home Screen" step.)

2. **Tap the link above** for your ball. You'll see the ball right away.

3. **Tap the Share button.** It's the square with an arrow pointing up, at the
   top of the screen.

4. **Scroll down the list and tap "Add to Home Screen."** (You may need to
   swipe up on the list of options to find it.)

5. **Tap "Add"** in the top corner. A ball icon now appears on your Home
   Screen, just like a regular app.

6. **Tap the ball icon once while you still have wifi.** It opens full-screen,
   like an app. This one launch is important — it saves the ball to the device
   so it never needs the internet again.

7. **Test it:** turn on Airplane Mode, tap the ball icon, and ask it a
   question. If it answers, you're set for the venue. Turn Airplane Mode off
   again.

That's it. The ball keeps the screen awake on its own, so you don't need to
touch any settings.

## Things worth knowing

- **Deleting the icon deletes the saved copy.** If you remove the ball from
  your Home Screen, just redo the steps above to get it back.
- **Don't turn on Low Power Mode on the big day.** The ball keeps your screen
  awake by itself, but Low Power Mode blocks that.
- **If the icon ever shows a plain white ball** instead of the striped 9-ball
  or the black 8-ball, the saved copy is old: delete the icon, connect to
  wifi, and redo the steps above.
- **Getting new answers later:** whenever we tell you the ball has new
  answers, just open the ball's icon once while connected to wifi, close it,
  and open it again. The new answers appear on that second opening.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Ball won't load at all | Check wifi, then reopen the link in Safari |
| Ball loads but won't answer with no wifi | Redo steps 1–7 (the save didn't complete) |
| Screen goes to sleep | Check that Low Power Mode is off (Settings → Battery) |
| Want it on another device? | Just redo steps 1–7 on that device |
